# Course Platform

A marketplace where instructors publish video courses and learners buy, watch and complete them.

## Language

### Refunds

**Refund Request**:
A learner's ask to get their money back for a purchase, reviewed by hand and ending as Approved or Declined. Learners raise one with "Cancel purchase".
_Avoid_: Chargeback, reversal, cancellation, claim

**Refund**:
The money given back to a learner when a Refund Request is approved. A declined request produces no Refund.
_Avoid_: Reversal, chargeback

**Refunded Purchase**:
A purchase whose Refund Request was approved; the learner no longer has access to the course.
_Avoid_: Cancelled purchase, reversed purchase

**Refund Window**:
The fourteen calendar days after a purchase during which a Refund Request may be raised.
_Avoid_: Cancel window, cancellation period

**Refund Eligibility**:
Whether a purchase may have a Refund Request raised right now: inside the Refund Window, little enough of the course watched or completed, not a claimed team seat, and no Refund Request already open.
_Avoid_: Cancellation offer

**Refundable Amount**:
What a learner would get back: the price they paid if they bought at a purchasing-power price, otherwise the course's list price.
_Avoid_: Reversal amount
